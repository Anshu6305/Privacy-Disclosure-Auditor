# References

Only sources that have actually been consulted appear here. Nothing is listed
to pad the bibliography, and no bibliographic detail is filled in from memory.

## Dataset

Wilson, S., Schaub, F., Dara, A. A., Liu, F., Cherivirala, S., Leon, P. G.,
Andersen, M. S., Zimmeck, S., Sathyendra, K. M., Russell, N. C., Norton, T. B.,
Hovy, E., Reidenberg, J., and Sadeh, N. "The creation and analysis of a website
privacy policy corpus." In *Proceedings of the 54th Annual Meeting of the
Association for Computational Linguistics*, Berlin, Germany, August 2016.

The OPP-115 corpus was developed by Carnegie Mellon University under the Usable
Privacy Policy Project. The annotations are made available for research,
teaching and scholarship purposes only, in the spirit of a Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial licence. Citation of the paper above is a condition
of use. Source of these terms: the `readme.txt` and
`documentation/manual.txt` files distributed with the corpus, read on
2026-11-04.

Corpus release used: v1.0, dated 2016-07-10 in its own version history. The
file-level checksums of the copy used by this project are recorded in
`ml/data/opp115_manifest.json`.

## Standards

ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148, *Systems and software engineering — Life cycle processes —
Requirements engineering*. Current edition 2018. This standard supersedes IEEE
Std 830-1998, which is superseded rather than withdrawn. The Phase 2 SRS uses
the IEEE 830-1998 section organisation with the 29148:2018 quality criteria, as
recorded in that document.

## Textbook

Rajib Mall, *Fundamentals of Software Engineering*. Used in Phase 3 for
structured analysis and structured design. Edition and page references are as
recorded in Report_Phase-03; no chapter or page number is cited here that was
not verified against the copy in hand.
